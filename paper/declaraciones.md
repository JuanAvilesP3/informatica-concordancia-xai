# Ethical and Policy Declarations (Informatica)

## 1. Disclosure of Generative AI and AI-Assisted Technologies
In accordance with *Informatica* editorial policy, the authors declare that large language models were utilized strictly as a controlled assistive tool for language editing, grammatical refinement, and LaTeX formatting. All research questions, experimental design, metric selections, empirical model execution, statistical analyses, and scientific interpretations were conceived, directed, and thoroughly validated by the human authors, who take full intellectual and ethical responsibility for the integrity of the published work.

## 2. Data and Code Availability
All underlying research outputs—including the four preprocessed public benchmark datasets (OULAD, Dropout, German Credit, Rice), trained machine learning model checkpoints, computed feature-attribution ranking matrices, Kendall's $\tau$ correlation tables, and complete Python scripts (`01_download.py` through `05_figures.py`)—are organized for full reproducibility and will be deposited in an open-access repository with a persistent Zenodo DOI upon publication.
