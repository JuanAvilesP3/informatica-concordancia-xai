# Ethical and Policy Declarations (Informatica)

## 1. Disclosure of Generative AI and AI-Assisted Technologies
In accordance with *Informatica* editorial policy (*"The use of LLMs and other language-support tools for proofreading and improving clarity is permitted. If generative AI materially contributed to writing, figures, analysis, or code, disclose this in the manuscript"*), the authors explicitly declare that large language models (Claude, Anthropic) were utilized as an assistive technology for drafting, code structuring, grammatical refinement, and LaTeX formatting. All research questions, experimental design, metric selections, empirical executions, statistical analyses, and scientific interpretations were conceived, directed, and thoroughly verified by the human authors, who take full and sole responsibility for the technical accuracy and integrity of the published work.

## 2. Data and Code Availability
